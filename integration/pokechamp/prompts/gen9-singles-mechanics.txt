GENERATION 9 SINGLES MECHANICS

Apply standard Pokemon Scarlet/Violet Generation 9 singles mechanics.

Major status:
- Burn: 1/16 max HP residual damage each turn and normally halves physical damage dealt.
- Paralysis: Speed is halved and each attempted action has a 25% chance of full paralysis.
- Poison: 1/8 max HP residual damage each turn.
- Toxic poison: damage increases each turn from 1/16, 2/16, 3/16, etc.; the Toxic counter resets on switching.
- Sleep: normally prevents actions for 1-3 sleep turns, subject to moves such as Sleep Talk.
- Freeze: normally prevents actions and has a 20% natural thaw chance each turn; some moves can thaw immediately.
- A Pokemon normally cannot have more than one major status condition at once.
- Confusion is volatile and disappears on switching.

Relevant type immunities include Fire to burn, Electric to paralysis, Poison/Steel to poison, Ice to freeze, and Grass to powder/spore moves, unless an explicit effect overrides the normal interaction.

Stat stages range from -6 to +6. +1 = 1.5x, +2 = 2x, -1 = 2/3x, and -2 = 1/2x. Most stat stages and volatile conditions reset when switching.

Move order is determined by priority first, then effective Speed. Do not assume a faster base Speed moves first if priority, paralysis, Choice Scarf, boosts, weather, or other effects alter the order.

Damage depends on more than base power. Account for attacking and defensive stats, stat stages, STAB, type effectiveness, abilities, items, weather, terrain, critical hits, and damage rolls.

Entry hazards:
- Stealth Rock damages switch-ins according to their Rock weakness/resistance; neutral damage is 1/8 max HP.
- Spikes damage grounded switch-ins: 1 layer = 1/8, 2 = 1/6, 3 = 1/4.
- Toxic Spikes poison grounded switch-ins; two layers badly poison them.
- Sticky Web lowers the Speed of grounded switch-ins by one stage.
- Heavy-Duty Boots normally prevent entry-hazard effects.

Switching normally removes stat changes, confusion, Substitute, and many other volatile effects, but HP loss and major status remain.

Weather, terrain, screens, hazards, residual damage, recovery, item removal, and field duration must be considered when evaluating future turns.

Each side may Terastallize once per battle. Terastallization changes defensive typing to the Tera type and can alter offensive STAB. Treat unused Tera as a limited strategic resource, not merely an immediate damage boost.

Do not assume unknown opponent moves, items, abilities, EVs, Tera type, or future actions. Apply special abilities, items, and move-specific exceptions only when they are confirmed or otherwise supplied by the battle state.
