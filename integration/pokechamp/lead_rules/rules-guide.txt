Lead Dex rules (dex-v2), added only after the original public preview cutoff:
$.lead_rules has version, self_slots, opponent_entries, species_facts and move_facts.
self_slots[] contains lead_slot (original submitted integer), species_ref (Dex ID), and supplied_move_refs (IDs for ONLY moves supplied for this slot). Never fill a missing move from another slot or from a species possibility list.
opponent_entries[] contains preview_index (1-based public position, not a self slot), public_details (unchanged), form_candidates (Dex IDs consistent with the public form), common_facts (only equal facts across those forms), and form_ambiguous (boolean). A wildcard form is not resolved using a hidden item or actor. Conditional species_facts remain available even when a form has no OU-legal possibilities.
species_facts maps species IDs to name, base_species, base_types (ordered names), non_tera_type_multipliers (attacking type -> product of the base type chart), type_status_immunities (brn/par/psn/tox/frz IDs), possible_abilities (IDs), possible_related_moves (IDs). The latter two are marginal Gen9 OU possibilities, not actual sets, simultaneous compatibility, completeness over all moves, adoption rates or lead probabilities. Self possibility lists never enlarge supplied_move_refs.
move_facts maps referenced move IDs to name, type, category, base_power, base_accuracy (percent or true for no ordinary accuracy check), priority, target, flags (Dex boolean/numeric flags), and related_categories. Categories are fixed: weather_change, hazard_removal_exchange, taunt_encore, pivot, priority_attack. Status Baton Pass is not an allowed OU pivot. Conditional priority/accuracy and move-specific exceptions are in effect_reference full descriptions; base values do not simulate a turn.
Type multipliers and status immunities describe non-Tera base types only, with ordinary type rules. They exclude ability/item/weather/move overrides and are not full damage calculations. For example, ability bypasses, immunity-changing moves, and post-Tera typing require their own conditions. Do not treat possible Magic Bounce as confirmed or unconditional: supplied Mold Breaker and the move's reflection/bypass conditions can matter.
All referenced move and possible ability descriptions are merged into effect_reference once per kind/ID. Presence in that dictionary never confirms possession. Descriptions mention other effects without recursively adding them.
