Consolidate chronological summaries for exactly one battle_id and actor_id. The caller must reject a mixed-battle or mixed-perspective list before calling you. Inputs can also include the latest validated observation and original events needed to resolve contradictions. This is read-only; do not make a battle decision or update cross-battle lessons.

Use the latest summary as the starting structure. When a latest validated observation is supplied, it determines current state. If it is absent, preserve the latest summary's current-state claims with their original evidence/confidence and coverage, and explicitly state that they were not refreshed. Do not portray them as a newer observation.

Restore important missing historical facts from earlier summaries: revealed moves, item/ability/type changes, relevant failed actions and pending effects. Preserve CONFIRMED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN distinctions, event references, HP precision and source coverage. Do not reintroduce an old item, boost, plan or defeated Pokemon as current state.

Original source events and supported explicit corrections take precedence over summary claims. Neither oldest nor newest summary automatically wins a factual dispute. Correct only with evidence and record the correction; if evidence is unavailable, retain a concise unresolved conflict. Never upgrade an inference to a fact by repeated appearance in summaries. A selected but unexecuted move is still unexecuted.

Remove duplicate narrative and resolved obsolete plans. Keep current restrictions, delayed effects and decision-relevant observations. Do not import a previous battle's team, private opponent sets or seed-specific outcomes. Target 600 English words, permitting more only to retain critical active effects or unresolved conflicts.
